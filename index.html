About JXI2
Established in 1995, JXI2 designs and manufactures the Syncclock line of precision timing cards for embedded and industrial computing systems.
Our products support multiple platforms including PCIex, VME, AMC, Mini PCIex, PMC and PC104-Plus, with configurable features such as GPS synchronization, OCXO-based timing stability, and IRIG-B output options for high-accuracy applications.
JXI2 delivers its solutions to aerospace, defense, and research customers worldwide through Brandywine Communications & Brandywine Communications UK.
